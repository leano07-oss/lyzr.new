02The problem

Procurement, held together by workarounds.

Procurement wasn’t struggling because the problems were new. It was fragmented, spread across tools, teams and inboxes never designed to work together.

  1. Manual, fragmented workflowsSupplier onboarding was tracked over email with no shared status. Spend data sat in dashboards with no direct path to action.
  2. Inconsistent, unauditable risk assessmentRisk assessments were rebuilt from scratch for every engagement, applying different standards with no audit trail.
  3. Knowledge that couldn’t scaleProcess knowledge lived in individual analysts. Not transferable, not reusable across the organization.
  4. Every engagement started from zeroNo shared infrastructure across engagements, so nothing built for one client carried over to the next.
03Native limits

Four requirements a real fix couldn’t skip.

Adding another point solution wasn’t going to fix a systems problem. The brief called for an operating layer, not a dashboard.

  1. Connect every stage, not just oneAn operating layer spanning the full procurement lifecycle, not a single-point tool.
  2. Coordinate automaticallyConnected agents that hand off work between steps on their own, not through manual handoffs.
  3. Stay configurable, not rigidFlows that adapt per client, category and region, instead of one fixed workflow.
  4. Be reusable, not rebuiltInfrastructure that serves every new client engagement without fresh engineering work each time.
04What Lyzr built

Four pillars, one platform.

The engagement was structured in phases. Phase 1 delivered the foundation in 12 weeks, with the strategic sourcing layer built in parallel.

Architecture pillars

  • Supplier management & onboardingOnboarding workflows, KYC, risk and supplier master data.
  • Strategic sourcing & supplier discoverySpend analysis, supplier discovery, RFQs and bid analysis.
  • Contract managementContract lifecycle, clause tracking and renewals.
  • Procurement operations & APRequisitions, PO tracking, invoice matching and AP automation.

Agent layer

  • Intake / journey agentInterprets requests and routes them to the correct flow.
  • Questionnaire agentSelects question sets based on category, region and risk profile.
  • Risk analysis agentScores responses, computes risk bands and updates the supplier profile.
  • Performance agentConfigures monitoring cadences, KPI definitions and alert thresholds per supplier.
05Architecture

One architecture, configured per engagement, not rebuilt.

The same infrastructure had to support many client engagements, each configured differently, without fresh engineering work every time.

06Controls & governance

Every decision, logged and explainable.

Nothing in the platform operates as a black box.

Traceability
Every agent action is logged: the trigger, the data used and the decision made.
Standardized scoring
Risk assessment applies the same 5-area framework across every engagement, not individual judgment.
Human decision gate
Agents surface comparisons and recommendations. Sourcing decisions are still made by people.
Configuration-driven
Templates, risk models and routing rules live in data, so changes don’t require new engineering work.
Security by design
Network isolation, least-privilege access and a web application firewall are built into every deployment.
