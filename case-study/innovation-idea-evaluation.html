02The problem

Where the weeks were going.

A dedicated team manually researched and evaluated every submitted idea, the same categories of work, redone from scratch, every time.

  1. Manual research, every timeA dedicated team researched and evaluated every idea manually, redoing the same category of work on each submission.
  2. Weeks per ideaEach evaluation took 1 to 2 weeks, regardless of how simple or complex the idea actually was.
  3. Inconsistent evaluation qualityManual assessment across 4 dimensions varied in depth and consistency from reviewer to reviewer.
  4. A bottleneck on decision-makingSlow evaluation cycles meant leadership couldn’t move quickly on the ideas that mattered most.
03Native limits

What a real fix had to do.

Speeding up part of the manual process wasn’t going to close a multi-week gap. Four things had to hold at once.

  1. Score every idea the same wayApply a consistent, standardized framework across market potential, competitive intensity, scalability and ecosystem fit, not reviewer judgment.
  2. Do real research, not templated outputPull actual market and competitive data for each submission, not generic boilerplate.
  3. Compress weeks into minutesRemove manual research time entirely, not just speed up parts of it.
  4. Keep leadership in the loopDeliver a report leadership reviews and decides on, not an automated approval.
04What Lyzr built

Five agents: 4 researchers, 1 summarizer.

Lyzr built an AI-agent workflow that researches each submitted idea against 4 fixed evaluation metrics, then consolidates the findings into a single scored report.

The 4 evaluation metrics

  • Market potentialMarket analysis and market-size estimates.
  • Competitive intensityCompetitor analysis and the competitive landscape.
  • ScalabilityHow far the idea scales.
  • Ecosystem fitHow the idea fits the surrounding ecosystem.

How the workflow runs

  • Sequential researchFour AI agents conduct the market and competitive research, one metric at a time.
  • Summary and scoringA summary agent consolidates the findings and produces numerical scores across the 4 metrics.
  • ReportThe output is a scored report, downloadable as a PDF.
05Architecture

One conversational intake, 4 sequential research calls, 1 report.

An employee submits an idea through a guided conversational intake. The system validates the submission, then triggers the evaluation workflows and a summary step that writes the results back to a central database.

06Controls

A structured score, not a number pulled from a black box.

Every report is built to withstand a leadership review.

Real-time validation
The conversational intake validates submissions before research begins, reducing incomplete or ambiguous inputs.
Weighted scoring model
A standardized, weighted framework computes pillar-level scores and a consolidated readiness score, the same way, every time.
Structured, traceable reports
Every report includes market analysis, market-size estimates, competitor analysis and pillar-level scores, not just a final number.
Human decision gate
The Innovation Review Team reviews every report and makes the go or no-go call. Nothing is auto-approved.
Re-evaluation on demand
If an idea is revised, the workflow re-triggers, updating research findings and recalculating scores.
07Re-imagined workflow

From a multi-week research assignment to one submission.

Before, an idea got assigned to a research team, who spent 1 to 2 weeks manually gathering the same categories of data every time. After, an employee submits the idea once, and a scored report is ready in 15 to 20 minutes.
